# Personas

## Privacy-conscious shopper (PCS)

**Role:** Shops with an AI agent while limiting what a merchant learns about their personal life.

**Context:** Understands the result of a shopping request but does not inspect code or network logs.

## Hackathon judge (HJ)

**Role:** Evaluates whether the WebMCP implementation works and whether its product claim is credible.

**Context:** May judge from the hosted app, repository, and short demo without configuring third-party accounts.

**Decision context:** Applies an initial viability check, then equally weights WebMCP leverage,
execution, potential impact, and creativity. May inspect browser behavior, code, or submission
materials, but is not required to build the project.
